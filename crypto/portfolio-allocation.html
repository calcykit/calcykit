Portfolio Allocation Calculator

Split your investment across any assets with percentage-based allocation. Add your own assets, set percentages, and instantly see exact dollar amounts for any portfolio size.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your total portfolio size — total capital to allocate
  2. Use a preset allocation or add your own assets
  3. Set the percentage for each asset — must total 100%
  4. See exact dollar amounts for each asset instantly
  5. Adjust percentages until allocation matches your strategy

Portfolio Allocation Formula

Allocation Formula
Asset Amount = Portfolio Size × (Allocation % / 100)

Example: $10,000 portfolio, 60% Bitcoin = $6,000 in Bitcoin

Rule: All allocation percentages must sum to exactly 100%

Portfolio Rebalancing

After setting your allocation, prices change and your portfolio drifts from target. Rebalancing restores your original percentages by selling overweight assets and buying underweight ones.

  • Threshold rebalancing — rebalance when any asset drifts 5%+ from target
  • Time-based rebalancing — rebalance on a fixed schedule (monthly, quarterly)
  • Cash flow rebalancing — direct new contributions to underweight assets

Frequently Asked Questions

What is portfolio allocation?+

Portfolio allocation is how you divide your investment capital across different assets. It determines your overall risk level, potential returns, and exposure to different markets. Your allocation should match your risk tolerance, investment horizon, and financial goals.

What is a good crypto portfolio allocation?+

A common beginner crypto allocation is 60% Bitcoin, 25% Ethereum, 15% altcoins. More conservative: 80% BTC, 20% ETH. More aggressive: 40% BTC, 30% ETH, 30% altcoins. Always adjust based on your risk tolerance and never invest more than you can afford to lose.

What is the 60/40 portfolio rule?+

The 60/40 rule allocates 60% to stocks and 40% to bonds. It is a classic balanced portfolio strategy offering growth through stocks and stability through bonds. It has historically delivered solid risk-adjusted returns with lower volatility than all-stock portfolios.

How often should I rebalance my portfolio?+

Most financial advisors recommend rebalancing quarterly or annually. In crypto, monthly rebalancing is common due to high volatility. Rebalancing restores your target allocation when assets drift due to price changes, keeping your risk level consistent.

What is diversification in investing?+

Diversification spreads your investment across different assets to reduce risk. When one asset falls, others may hold or rise, reducing overall portfolio volatility. It does not eliminate risk but significantly reduces it — especially important in volatile crypto markets.
