Crypto P&L Formula

Profit / Loss Formula
P&L = (Sell Price × Qty - Sell Fee) - (Buy Price × Qty + Buy Fee)

Buy Fee = Buy Price × Qty × (Fee % / 100)

Sell Fee = Sell Price × Qty × (Fee % / 100)

ROI = (Net Profit / Total Buy Cost) × 100

Why Fees Matter More Than You Think

On a $10,000 trade at 0.1% fee each way you pay $20. That seems small. But if you make 100 trades per year that is $2,000 in fees — money directly taken from your profit. Always use the lowest fee exchange for your trading volume.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate crypto profit and loss?+

Crypto P&L = (Sell Price - Buy Price) × Quantity - Total Fees. If the result is positive you made a profit. If negative you made a loss. Always include fees from both sides of the trade for accurate results.

What fees should I include in crypto P&L?+

Include both the buy fee and sell fee from your exchange. Most exchanges charge 0.1% to 0.6% per trade. Check your exchange fee schedule and enter both sides for accurate profit calculation.

How is crypto ROI calculated?+

Crypto ROI = (Net Profit / Total Amount Invested) × 100. Total invested includes the buy cost plus buy fee. Net profit is sell revenue minus sell fee minus total invested.

Does this calculator work for all cryptocurrencies?+

Yes. This calculator works for Bitcoin, Ethereum, BNB, Solana, or any cryptocurrency. Just enter the buy price, sell price, quantity, and fees in your preferred currency.

How do crypto trading fees affect profit?+

Fees directly reduce your profit. On a $10,000 trade at 0.1% fee each way, you pay $20 in fees. Over many trades this compounds significantly. Always factor fees into your profit calculation before deciding to trade.
